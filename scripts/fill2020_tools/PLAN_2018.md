# 2018 open rev cells — measured route plan + outcomes (campaign 2026-08-11)

**NO ASSUMPTIONS, NO GUESSWORK.** Every value written and every claim below traces to a document or
a measurement made this campaign; unknown stays unknown. (Runbook §0 golden rule.)

Method and tooling inherited from `PLAN_2019.md` (campaign 2026-08-10, 34 cells landed). This doc
records only what 2018 MEASURED differently — and the two places where the 2019 method turned out
to be wrong.

**292 companies, 730 cells** — 2.9× the 2019 set (254). `na` is already excluded by the coverage

is sparse — and con PAT gates con revenue).

## ★★★ THE 2019 METHOD WAS WRONG ABOUT "NOT APPLICABLE" — E6, and 157 records it would have written

The 2019 campaign recorded 61 cells as **not-applicable** on E1+E2+E3 (runbook §54b): NSE's
per-company index lists a standalone row for the quarter, no consolidated one, and the quarter
precedes the company's FIRST consolidated row ever. Run unchanged over the 2018 set that rule
produces **336 not-applicable cells** — 46% of the target list.

**It is wrong, and 2018 is big enough to prove it.** Of those 336, **100 have a stored consolidated
PAT for the very quarter being called unfiled, and 98 of those differ MATERIALLY from the
standalone one**:

| cell | stored con PAT | stored std PAT | NSE index first-con |
|---|---|---|---|
| AXISBANK 2018-06 | 721.86 | 701.09 | 2019-06-30 |
| BANKBARODA 2018-09 | 603.71 | 425.38 | 2019-03-31 |
| GAIL 2018-09 | 1,787.16 | 1,962.96 | 2019-06-30 |
| AARTIIND 2018-09 | 138.58 | 122.92 | 2019-06-30 |

A distinct consolidated number had to be READ FROM A DOCUMENT. So for these companies the index's
pre-first-con silence is **measured-incomplete**, not evidence of non-filing — exactly §57's rule
("a route returning nothing means THAT ROUTE has no row") and §63's ("never infer *the company
doesn't file it* from a gap"). E3 is not vacuous in general — 185 of the 292 target companies
(63.4%) DO have pre-2019 consolidated rows in the index, some back to 2005 — but it is falsified
for any company whose own stored figures contradict its first-con date.

**E6, the new gate:** *the index's pre-first-con silence is evidence only if our own store holds NO
**materially different** consolidated figure **strictly before** that first-con date.*

Both bounds were arrived at by getting them wrong first, and both change the verdict:

* **STRICTLY BEFORE, not at-or-before.** A company whose index first-con is Jun-2019 — the quarter
  consolidated quarterlies became compulsory (§51a) — and whose Jun-2019 filing shows con ≠ std has
  demonstrated only that it HAS subsidiaries, not that it filed a consolidated quarterly earlier.
  That is the textbook §51a case and E3 stands. The first cut of E6 counted the first-con quarter
  itself and wrongly retracted 27 such cells (BAJAJCON, BEL, BHEL, CANBK, CONCOR, DMART…).
* **MATERIALLY DIFFERENT, not merely present.** Earlier passes in this repo manufactured con PAT by
  COPYING std (§54b's circularity warning), so a stored con equal to std proves nothing.

Result:

* 2018 na set: **336 → 179 cells**, all with null con PAT. E6 refutes **157**.
* ★★★ **AND E6 WAS STILL NOT ENOUGH — E7.** E6 only asks whether OUR OWN store contradicts the
  index, which is a claim about our data. Of the 179 cells it left standing, **52 were then FILLED
  from Moneycontrol** — series reproducing 26-32 of our stored quarters with ZERO disagreements,
  publishing a consolidated revenue for the very quarter the record called unfiled. Among them
  BHARATFORG, BATAINDIA, BOMDYEING: companies that self-evidently file consolidated accounts.
  **E7: a cell may not be recorded as NEVER-FILED until an independent reader has been tried and has
  ALSO come up empty** — the exact mirror of §60f's rule for "unfillable", of which only the positive
  direction had ever been written down. **179 → 118 cells** (`scripts/no_con_quarterly_2018.json`).
  Runbook §82d.
* **Convergent finding, independently.** While this ran, another session pushed `fe5af03e`
  ("FILL-2019: the aggregator rung I skipped") diagnosing the SAME defect from the other end — a
  screener-derived annual identity produced consolidated values for INDUSINDBK and NHPC, both of
  which were sitting in `no_con_quarterly_2019.json` as not-applicable. Their test (any stored con
  rev/PAT for an EARLIER quarter) refuted 26 of the 61 and kept them visible under
  `refuted_2026_08_11`. **Their work is preserved as-is**; E6 was applied on top of their surviving
  35 and adds exactly **one** more — **SWANCORP 2019-03**, which stores a con PAT (−38.66) *for the
  very quarter* the record calls unfiled. An earlier-quarters-only test cannot see that; the
  target-quarter check is E6's independent contribution.
* **Nothing in the data changes** — every record carries `written: null`, and grep confirms no tool
  consumes that file (the ledger that feeds the coverage definition is the separate
  `scripts/no_con_filing.json`, which contains none of them). What was wrong was the CLAIM.

## ★★ 36 CELLS WERE NEVER ATTEMPTED — the delisted-scrip drop (§52b × §57a rule 4)

`bse_scrips.json` is built from BSE's LIVE scrip master, so a delisted company resolves to nothing
and `backfill_revop_gaps` drops it from the worklist **before fetching anything**. Measured on the
anchored pool: **19 companies / 36 cells**, including ALBK, DHFL, FRETAIL, MINDTREE, RELCAPITAL,
RELINFRA, IDFC, SREINFRA, UJJIVAN, EQUITAS. Those are *not attempted*, and reporting them as
residue would read as "tried and failed".

Fixed with `fill2020_tools/resolve_delisted_scrips.py`, which resolves them from
`_bse_master_all.json` **gated on ISIN** — never on `scrip_id`, which is the KALYANI coincidence
(§76). The ISIN NSE prints on its own filing-index rows (`isin`) is compared against the master's
`ISIN_NUMBER`: **29 companies resolved**, 3 rejected for having no master row at all (CDSL,
MAXINDIA, RNAVAL).

**One refinement the gate itself forced.** Strict full-ISIN equality rejected CORPBANK
(INE112A01015 vs INE112A01023) and HDFC (INE001A01028 vs INE001A01036) — same company, security
re-issued after a face-value change, which moves the ISIN's issue suffix while leaving the ISSUER
untouched. Matching on the 7-char issuer prefix admits those two and still refuses KALYANI
(INE610E vs INE0N6U — the issuer itself differs), which is the trap the gate exists for.
`backfill_revop_gaps.py` now consults the override map.

## ★★ PSEUDO-ANCHORED CELLS — "has a con PAT" is not the same as "has an anchor"

§64's split asks whether a con PAT is stored. That is necessary but not sufficient: if the stored
con PAT **equals the standalone one**, it cannot tell a reader which basis a column belongs to, and
on a combined "Standalone and Consolidated" page the reader lands the standalone column — §76b's
trap, which is how CORPBANK 2019-03 produced a wrong value. **15 of the 336 anchored 2018 revC cells
are in that state** (GICRE ×3, 3MINDIA ×2, LUXIND ×2, ARE&M, BALRAMCHIN, CEMPRO, HAVELLS,
HEROMOTOCO, ICICIPRULI, PNB, ZFCVINDIA). They should be expected to fail the cross-basis gate, not
counted as reachable. Recorded per cell as `pseudo_anchored` in `_class_rev2018.json`.

### ★★★ GICRE: the §55c COPY class, now bounded by measurement

The 2019 campaign flagged that GICRE's stored con PAT equals std to the paisa in all four 2019
quarters. Measured over GICRE's whole series:

    2016-06 .. 2021-12   con PAT == std PAT, 23 CONSECUTIVE quarters
    2022-03 .. 2026-03   con PAT differs from std by 0.24% .. 44.4%, every quarter, never returning

A real consolidated series does not switch behaviour like that on one date, and GIC Re has
subsidiaries throughout. The clean break at Mar-2022 is exactly where §55c found and fixed the
defect for 17 cells. **So every GICRE con PAT before 2022-03 is an artefact, not an anchor**, and
GICRE's three 2018 revC cells are held on that basis rather than read — anchoring on a copied value
would return the standalone column and manufacture a consolidated figure. This bounds the pre-2022
sweep §55c already queues: 23 quarters, 2016-06 → 2021-12.

## ★★★ MONEYCONTROL — the route that actually moved 2018, and the cap that nearly hid it

Handed over mid-campaign by the 2019 session. One request per (symbol, basis) returns the company's
whole quarterly series at **filing precision** under `Net Sales/Income from operations` — our exact
basis. Crucially it needs **no PAT anchor**, so it is the only route that reaches the 389 cells §64
blocks outright.

### ⚠️ `limit` IS A SILENT CAP, AND `count` MIRRORS IT
The endpoint takes `&limit=N` and the payload's `count` echoes whatever you asked for, so a
truncated series reads as *the site's own reach* when it is really your own parameter
(memory: `feedback-endpoint-caps-are-silent`). Measured on DLF consolidated, 2026-08-11:

    limit=60   -> 60 rows, count:60, oldest Sep-2011
    limit=200  -> 77 rows, count:77, oldest Jun-2007

**This campaign published a wrong "measured" boundary because of it.** A first pass at the shallower
limit produced fill rates of 4% for Mar-2018 against 27–36% for Jun/Sep/Dec, and that was written up
as *"MC's consolidated series starts at Jun-2018 for many names"* — a number that was an artefact of
the request. Caught by the 2019 session mid-run; the run was killed, `scripts/_mc_qcache` deleted
(**the cache key did not include the limit**, so a 60-row body would have been silently reused and
the extra rows would never have appeared), and everything re-fetched at depth. Values already landed
were unaffected — the cap changes reach, not row values; 3MINDIA re-read 720.43 / 746.00 identically.

### The boundary, re-measured at full depth — and it is real
`no row for 20180331` remains the dominant Mar-2018 refusal (37 cells) *at limit=200*, so this time
it is a property of the data. Two distinct causes, both measured on the series themselves:

* **APOLLOHOSP** — 32 **contiguous** quarters beginning exactly at 20180630, no internal gaps.
* **BALRAMCHIN** — 34 quarters, but only isolated singletons at 20070930 and 20080930 before a
  contiguous run from 20180630 (two internal gaps of ~1 and ~10 years).

So Moneycontrol's **consolidated** quarterly series effectively begins at **Jun-2018 = Q1 FY2019**,
with scattered older singletons — the §51a compulsion shape once more. **Mar-2018 is the last
quarter before that boundary**, which is why it is the hardest of the four and why the campaign's
Mar-2018 coverage lags the other three quarters on every route, not just this one.

### The gate did its job on the insurers
MC's series for GICRE / HDFCLIFE / ICICIPRULI reproduced **0 of our stored quarters with 10–25
disagreements** (e.g. HDFCLIFE 2019-12: ours 11,701.69 vs mc 7,854.98), so §60c rejected all four
companies outright rather than cherry-picking. That is correct, not a failure: insurers file in
IRDAI format and `Net Sales/Income from operations` is simply not the quantity we store for them
(§55). Those cells stay with the §55/§43 insurer routes.

## ★★★ THE SECOND READER, AND WHY A DISAGREEMENT IS A THREAD NOT A VERDICT

§60f says a cell may not be called unfillable until a second reader has been tried. The mirror:
**a cell is not FILLED until a second reader has had the chance to contradict it.** MC contradicted
5 of ours. Exactly ONE was ours to fix — treating the second reader as an oracle would have
overwritten four correct values.

| cell | verdict | arbitrated by |
|---|---|---|
| FINCABLES 2018-09 732.20 → **713.97** | **WE WERE WRONG** | the filing: `Revenue from Operations 713.97` / `Total Income (I+II) 732.20` in the 30-Sep-18 column. Row I's figures extract on a line of their own BEFORE the label while row III carries label AND figures, so the label matched and the numbers came from the wrong row (§75b). **A 2.5% error is invisible to the neighbour band by construction** |
| SADBHAV 2018-12 1,308.06 | **OURS** | §45: printed 9M = 384,894.13 lakh = 3,848.94 cr, and 1,495.07 + 1,045.81 + 1,308.06 = 3,848.94 exactly; MC's three sum to 3,768.14 |
| MCLEODRUSS 2018-12 553.10 | **OURS** | the filing prints 55,310 lakh in the 31-Dec-2018 column |
| CHAMBLFERT 2018-12 2,785.68 | OURS, contested | MC disagrees with 2 of our 3 stored FY19 siblings and overshoots screener's annual by 82.36 |
| SHRIRAMFIN 2018-12 4,115.92 | OURS | MC reproduces only 7 of ours with 26 disagreements — not credible here |

Surfaced and **deliberately not patched** (§58d — report, never silently patch): CARERATING 2018-06,

## ★★★ THE RETRACTION — an aggregator's consolidated fallback (2026-08-11)

Flagged by the 2019 session mid-campaign. Moneycontrol returns a `cons_quarterly` row for a company
**even in quarters where no consolidated result was filed**, and in those quarters the row carries
the **standalone** figure.

**Every guard this campaign ran missed it.** The §60c series gate proves MC's consolidated series IS
this company's consolidated series by reproducing our stored con values elsewhere — and it does,
correctly, on 26-32 quarters. The fallback is PER QUARTER inside a series that is otherwise right.
The PAT anchor, §54a's magnitude band and §76b's cross-basis gate all pass.

**Two of my own positions had to go:**
* `screen_crossbasis_2018.py` was too lenient — it cleared a `con == std` cell when the agreeing
  neighbours outnumbered the differing ones. The correct rule: if the company consolidates
  differently in **any** quarter, an identical figure here is the source repeating standalone.
* I nearly exempted 28 of 29 on the grounds that every differing quarter falls **after** the target
  ("it acquired subsidiaries later"). **That is circular**: our pre-2019 consolidated coverage is
  exactly what this campaign is filling, so there CAN BE no differing quarter before a 2018 target.
  Backward evidence does not exist by construction — the same circularity that sank E3.

**28 retracted** (annotated `held`, nothing deleted); **21 kept**, 20 on "never differs anywhere" and
**one on primary evidence**: FINCABLES 2018-09, where the standalone (p3) and consolidated (p8)
statements in one filing print the identical revenue row, differing only in Other Income.

⚠️ **A process trap worth not repeating.** The first retraction commit pushed the annotated ledger
but wrote ZERO cells: the screen had been re-run in dry mode after applying, which overwrote its
output file with an empty hold list, and the push script read that. Origin briefly carried the wrong
values while the ledger said they were held. **Drive a retraction from the DATA, never from a report
file the tool rewrites on every run.** `verify_fills_live.py` then caught a second instance —
SYNGENE was claimed by a *different* ledger too. **When a cell is retracted, every ledger that
claims it must be annotated, not just the route you retracted from.**

## ★★★ THE VISION RUNG — 15 of 15, and the triage that made it cheap

The diagnostic labels a cell from the documents in ITS OWN window — the company's own filing for
that quarter — and for a pre-2019 con cell that is the wrong document twice over (§84: BSE no longer
serves pre-Oct-2018 attachments; §51a: a 2018 annual filing routinely carries consolidated ANNUAL
only, verified under vision on GAIL's Mar-2019 filing). The document that carries a 2018 quarter's
consolidated figure is the **year-later filing**, which is post-Oct-2018 and therefore fetchable and
often text-bearing. **4 of the 15 needed no vision at all.**

**Not one value rests on reading a number off a scan.** Every cell carries an independent control:
UNIONBANK five exact controls each; HINDUNILVR's own Dec-19/Sep-19 columns reproduce stored revC
exactly *and* fix the products+services+other-operating summation rule; RECLTD's PBT − tax + deferred
= 1,284.00 == stored con PAT; PFC's Dec-2019 columns reproduce **both** stored bases.

⚠️ **A 200dpi render is not a read.** On MRPL at 200dpi the CON Dec-2019 cell read `16,417.86`
against a stored 16,417.66 and the STD PAT `(287.72)` against −267.72 — two digit errors on one page,
while standalone revenue matched exactly both years. At 420dpi in a tight crop it resolves to
16,417.66. Every subsequent cell was read at 380–430dpi cropped to the header + target row.
