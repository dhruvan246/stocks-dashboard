## Round-2 close: the residue is dominated by SCANS, not by attachment selection

Re-measured the `no-pl-page` cells instead of trusting the label (`_noplpage_diag.json`), because
the classification itself was wrong:

**The diagnostic's scannedness test was "did ANY document have text", and that hid 8 cells.**
DLF Mar-2019 lists three result-flagged announcements — a 4-page compliance letter WITH a text
layer, plus the two that matter, "Audited Financial Results" (30 pages) and the Q1 results
(24 pages), both with **ZERO characters**. The compliance letter satisfied `ntext > 0`, so the cell
reported `no-pl-page`, which reads as "wrong attachment, fixable by selection" when the truth is
"every results document is an image". Fixed: scannedness is now judged on documents that could
plausibly carry a statement (>=8 pages), by characters per page. DLF now reports
`scanned-results-docs (0 chars/page)`.

**Measured over the 17 `no-pl-page` cells: 8 are effectively SCANS** (DLF 0 · PFC 0930 190 ·
BALKRISIND 259 · DHANI 0630 257 · DHANI 0331 264 · MINDACORP 350 · ADANIPORTS 447 · MANAPPURAM 598
chars/page) **and 9 carry real text** (1,800-3,500 chars/page) but no page matching either
"revenue from operations" or "Interest Earned".

**Also ruled out, by measurement:** the 10-attachment scan cap is NOT the limiter — every sampled
cell has only 2-6 result announcements in the qe+8d..qe+160d window and all were read. And
`FI.datebound` already queries `strCat=-1` (all categories), so the category filter is not the
limiter either. §55e's "select by content" therefore is NOT what these cells need.

### The blocker, stated honestly
**14 of the ~67 still-open cells in this subset need the VISION rung** — 8 scanned results
documents plus the 6 glyph-corrupted text layers whose DIGITS are mangled. That is now the single
largest cause, ahead of every reader fix. Per the standing rule the vision rung is expensive and
gets asked about first, so it is **not attempted here**.

The 9 text-bearing `no-pl-page` cells split further: ANDHRABANK 0630, BANKINDIA, KOTAKBANK,
MUTHOOTFIN ×2 and PFC 0630 are BANKS whose pages the reader now understands but whose **printed
header dates it cannot detect** ("column-by-date: no printed dates in the header") — a bounded,
non-vision lever worth taking next.
