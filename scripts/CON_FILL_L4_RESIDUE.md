## Read this before treating any row as a gap

The dominant class is not a data gap at all. **105 of the 211 cells are the quarter ended
31-Mar-2018, and consolidated quarterly reporting became compulsory only from FY2020** — for FY2019
a filer could opt out under SEBI LODR Reg 33(3)(b)(i), and most of these did. Measured per name, not
assumed: readers opened each company's **own Q4FY18 filing** (the filing FOR the target quarter),
its **Q1FY19 filing** (whose column set includes "preceding 3 months ended 31.03.2018"), and its
**Q4FY19 filing** (the year-later comparative). The consistent finding is that the Q4FY18 filings
print consolidated on YEAR columns only while the standalone block beside them carries the quarter,
and the Q1FY19 filings are standalone-only. Seven companies say so in their own words — NMDC,
SUNDRMFAST and PNCINFRA in filing notes, HEG / JBCHEPHARM / KNRCON / NLCINDIA in their Reg 33(3)(b)(i)
intimation letters. RELIANCE is the counter-example that proves the method rather than the rule: it
DID publish a consolidated Q4FY18 quarter column, and that cell filled.

⚠️ The first pass reached these conclusions with one rung untried, because BSE serves no attachment
filed before ~Oct-2018 (§84). **74 of those verdicts were re-opened and re-read** once it was
measured that NSE's announcement archive serves the same 2018 documents (§101). The re-read changed
labels but not outcomes: the door is open, and the value is not behind it.

## The two refusal labels

* `NO_CONSOLIDATED_FILED` — the filing for that quarter contains no consolidated statement, or the
  company states it publishes consolidated annually only. The strongest class.
* `COMPARATIVE_ONLY` — a consolidated figure for the PERIOD exists (typically the FY18 annual), but
  the QUARTER is printed nowhere reachable, and no consolidated 9M column exists to derive it from.
  Materially the same outcome; the difference is whether any consolidated statement exists at all.
* `NOT_REACHED` — the ladder is NOT exhausted. One cell only, and it is named as such rather than
  buried in a refusal count.
