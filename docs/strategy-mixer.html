🎛️ Strategy Mixer — blend strategies into one portfolio

Pick a few of your saved strategies, decide how many rupees go into each, and see how the combined portfolio would have done — growth, worst fall, how much the strategies overlap, and whether mixing them actually made the ride smoother. Choose whether each strategy runs on its own money or everything goes into one shared basket (where a stock two strategies both pick gets double the money). Every strategy runs over the same dates with the same engine as its own backtest page.

📒 Trade log — every position the mix took

The rebalance and trade logs are each strategy's own, tagged and merged — pick one strategy in either dropdown to read it exactly as its backtest page shows it. A trade's sell price on a "still held" row is the last available price in the window. Separate buckets: each strategy runs exactly like its own backtest — same engine, same survivorship-free prices, rebalancing its own stocks on its own schedule — on the rupees you gave it, and the mix is simply the sum of those buckets marked month by month, with money never shifted between strategies. One combined portfolio: the same strategy signals, but the whole amount sits in a single basket — each strategy asks for as many slots as it holds names, a stock several strategies want fills several slots and carries that multiple of the money, and the entire portfolio is re-spread to the new plan (at its current value) whenever any strategy rebalances. A strategy holding nothing keeps its share in cash rather than crowding the others. Market cap in the portfolio table is today's, not the company's size on the rebalance date — the survivorship-free price history these runs use carries prices, not share counts, so no point-in-time market cap exists; large/mid/small follow the usual top-100 / next-150 / rest split on that same current ranking. Brokerage, taxes and slippage are not modelled; a delisted stock's position is written down to zero. Benchmark = Nifty 500 total of the same total capital (Nifty 50 when 500 history is unavailable).
