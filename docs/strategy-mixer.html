How the mix is computed: each strategy runs exactly like its own backtest — same engine, same survivorship-free prices, rebalancing its own stocks on its own schedule — on the rupees you gave it, and the mix is simply the sum of those buckets marked month by month. Money is never shifted between strategies. Brokerage, taxes and slippage are not modelled; a delisted stock's position is written down to zero. Benchmark = Nifty 500 total of the same total capital (Nifty 50 when 500 history is unavailable).
