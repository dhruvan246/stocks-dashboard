How these numbers are made

One price store for the whole page. Stock prices are split / bonus adjusted closes from the official NSE and BSE daily files, plus the live price in market hours (NSE's whole-market feed, BSE's a few minutes behind). Today = the latest price against the previous close, and only for a stock that has a price on the day shown — a stock that has not traded that day shows a dash. 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / 3Y = against the close 7 / 30 / 91 / 182 / 365 / 1,095 calendar days earlier (or the last one before that, at most 10 days older); YTD = against the last close of the previous year. Dividends are not adjusted.

Sectors. Four levels of the exchanges' industry classification. A group's figure is the plain average of its stocks' returns (every stock counts the same); a group needs 5 stocks with a figure to be listed. All stocks is every stock that passes the filters. The group chart is that same average chained day by day from a start of 100, using today's members. Top movers is the same list opened on All stocks → Gainers with a ₹100 Cr floor and BSE-only and SME names included. Market-cap bands: Large ₹20,000 Cr and up, Mid ₹5,000–20,000 Cr, Small below that. RS ranks a stock's blended return (20% 1-month + 40% 3-month + 40% 6-month) among all stocks of ₹500 Cr and up, 1 (weakest) to 99. Median P/E uses trailing-twelve-month earnings.

Indices. Level and Today are live from NSE's index feed in market hours; indices the live feed does not carry are marked CLOSE and read from NSE's daily closing file. 1W / 1M / 1Y come from NSE's publication where it has them; 3M / 6M / YTD / 3Y, every rewound figure and the CLOSE rows are computed from closing values. PE, PB and dividend yield are NSE's own; the small number beside PE / PB is where today's figure sits in that index's own observed range (100 = dearest seen). Members are NSE's list — today's, or with Rewind the list in force on that date.

Rewind moves the whole page to the close of a past date: prices, returns, RS, index levels and member lists are as of that date; market cap is the cap on that date (≈ = estimated from today's share count); F&O, Nifty 500 and Microcap 250 are the lists in force that day; the industry classification and the SME lists are today's. Companies that have since left our price data are not shown. Educational only — not investment advice.
