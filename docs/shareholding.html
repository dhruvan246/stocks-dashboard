How these numbers are made

Source: NSE shareholding-pattern XBRL filings (SEBI format). FII = Institutions (Foreign): FPI category I+II, foreign direct investment & other foreign institutions. DII = Institutions (Domestic): mutual funds, insurance, banks, pension/provident, AIFs etc. MF = mutual funds alone and Insurance = insurance companies alone (both are part of DII). Δ columns are the change in percentage points (pp) vs the company's previous filed quarter; “first” means there is no earlier quarter on file to compare with.

The quarter picker shows every company's holding for that quarter (default: each company's latest); Filed on shows only filings submitted on that date. The index filter uses each NSE index's current member list (not membership as of the quarter shown). The FII streak filter (🔥) counts consecutive quarters — counting back from the quarter shown — in which FII stake rose by at least 0.05 pp; a missed or non-consecutive quarter ends the streak.

Holdings are disclosed quarterly (within 21 days of quarter end) — companies file on different days, so new rows appear here every day during filing season; this page refreshes twice daily. The price chart is the stock's daily price history from this site's own store. Educational only — not investment advice.
