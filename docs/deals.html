How these numbers are made

Every disclosed bulk deal (a client trading >0.5% of a company's shares in a day) and block deal (large negotiated trades in the special block windows) on NSE. Source: NSE bulk & block deal disclosures, published every trading evening (~6:30 pm IST) and accumulated here into a rolling ~92-day window that grows daily. A trade shows on both sides only when both parties crossed the disclosure threshold, so total buys ≠ total sells. Deal value = quantity × trade/weighted-average price.

Click a client's name in the middle pane to see all their deals. For a stock, net bought = buys − sells over the deals matching your filters; buyers counts the different clients on the buy side. The chart is the stock's daily price history from this site's own store. Educational only — not investment advice.
