📖 Glossary — every code on this page, spelled out (rank-by factors, filters, columns)

How to read one row

Rank by is written direction-factor. high-ret6m = sort every surviving stock by 6-month return, biggest first. low-upPct = sort by up-day consistency, smallest first. The top 3 or 5 of that sorted list is what the strategy buys.

Filters are the entry conditions, all of them ANDed, applied before the ranking. d52<=25 & fiiChgPp>0 = keep only stocks within 25% of their 52-week high that also had FIIs adding last quarter, then rank whatever is left. A stock missing a factor's value is dropped, never ranked last.

Rebalance happens on the last trading day of every month. Monthly reset = the whole basket is sold and re-bought equal-weight each month. Ride winners (hold) = only names that fell out of the top N are sold; survivors are kept untouched (so winners compound and weights drift), and the freed cash is split equally among the empty slots.

Universe is point-in-time and survivorship-free: a stock is eligible on a date only if it was actually in the Nifty 500 (or in F&O, for the F&O variant) on that date, and delisted companies stay in history. Fundamentals and shareholding are point-in-time too — a quarter is only visible after its real filing date.

Result columns

CAGR %
Compound annual growth rate — the annualised return over that window.
Total %
Absolute return over the window, not annualised. Shown as x above 1,000% (12.4x = the money multiplied 12.4 times).
Max DD %
Maximum drawdown — the worst peak-to-trough fall of the strategy's own equity curve. Amber above 35%.
Win %
Share of monthly rebalance periods that ended positive.
Worst rank
The strategy's poorest rank across all 4 phases (or all calendar years), out of 44.4 lakh. Low = never bad in any regime, which is the point of the ⭐ cards.

Price & momentum factors

ret1m
Return — 1 month %. Price today vs price 30 days ago.
ret3m
Return — 3 month %. Price today vs 91 days ago.
ret6m
Return — 6 month %. Price today vs 182 days ago.
ret12m
Return — 12 month %. Price today vs 365 days ago.
accel
Momentum acceleration. This month's 1-month return minus last month's, in percentage points. Positive = the move is speeding up.
riskMom
Risk-adjusted momentum. 3-month return ÷ annualised volatility. Rewards a steady climb over a violent one.
postDrift
Post-result drift %. Return since the company's last earnings announcement date — the classic post-earnings drift.
composite
Quality-Momentum composite. Sum of three same-day cross-sectional z-scores: TTM profit growth (+), 12-month return (+), volatility (−). 0 = an average stock that day, ≥2 = strong on all three at once.

Trend & price levels

d52
Distance from 52-week High %, as a positive number (Trendlyne convention). High 100, price 95 → 5. So d52<=10 means "within 10% of its one-year high".
d52_low_pct
Distance from 52-week Low % — how far above the low it trades. 100 = it has doubled off the low.
rangePos
52-week range position. Where the price sits between the year's low (0) and high (100).
daysHigh
Days since the 52-week high. Low = it just made a new high; high = a long time since.
dma50
Distance from the 50-DMA % — % above (+) or below (−) the average close of the last 50 days.
dma200
Distance from the 200-DMA % — same, against the 200-day average.
indRank
Industry momentum rank. On each rebalance date, industries are ranked by their members' average 1-month return and bucketed into deciles: 1 = hottest industry, 10 = coldest.

Risk

vol
Volatility %. Standard deviation of daily returns over the last 90 days, annualised.
beta
Beta vs Nifty. Slope of the stock's daily returns against the Nifty's over the last 120 days. 1 = moves with the index, >1 = amplifies it.
mdd6
Max drawdown — 6 month %. Worst peak-to-trough fall in the stock over the last 182 days.
upPct
Up-day consistency %. Of the ~62 trading sessions in the last 90 days, the share that closed higher than the previous close. High = a steady grind; low = the gains (or losses) came in a few jumps.

Liquidity & participation

turnover
Average daily turnover in ₹ lacs over 20 days (₹100 lacs = ₹1 Cr). turnover>=2000 = at least ₹20 Cr traded a day.
turnSurge
Turnover surge. Last week's average turnover ÷ the last 90 days'. 1.5 = money flowing in 50% heavier than usual.
volSurge
Volume surge — the same ratio measured on share volume instead of rupees.
delivPct
Delivery %. Average share of traded volume actually taken to demat (not intraday) over the last 28 days. High = real buying, not churn. Data exists from 2020 onward.

Oscillators

rsi
RSI(14) — Wilder's Relative Strength Index on daily closes. Below 30 = oversold, above 70 = overbought.
macd
MACD histogram (12,26,9) — MACD line minus its signal line. Above 0 = short-term trend is running ahead of the medium-term one.
stoch
Stochastic %K(14) — where the close sits inside the last three weeks' price range, 0 to 100.
bollB
Bollinger %b (20,2) — where the close sits between the lower band (0) and the upper band (100).

Fundamentals — point-in-time, consolidated

profitYoyPct
Net profit growth, quarter YoY %. Latest filed quarter's net profit vs the same quarter a year earlier. A loss-making year-ago base is divided by its absolute value, so a swing from −10 Cr to +5 Cr reads +150%, not a nonsense number.
profitBase
Year-ago quarter's net profit, ₹Cr — the denominator itself. profitBase>0 filters out the optical growth you get off a loss-making base.
profitAccel
Profit-growth acceleration. This quarter's YoY growth minus last quarter's, in points. Positive = growth is improving, not just positive.
profitTTM
TTM profit growth %. Sum of the last 4 quarters vs the sum of the 4 before them — smooths out seasonality.
profitStreak
Profit-growth streak. Consecutive quarters of positive YoY growth, counting back from the latest filing.

Ownership — quarterly shareholding filings

fiiPct
FII holding % — foreign institutional stake in the latest filed quarter.
fiiChgPp
FII change QoQ, in percentage points, vs the immediately preceding quarter only. fiiChgPp>0 = foreigners added. The Sep-2022 SEBI reclassification is skipped, since it moved stakes on paper without anyone buying.
diiPct
DII holding % — domestic institutions (MFs, insurers, banks) in the latest filed quarter.
diiChgPp
DII change QoQ, in percentage points — same rules as FII.

Fine print

Every lookback above is counted in calendar days, so "90 days" is about 62 trading sessions. Returns are computed on raw traded prices. Brokerage, STT, slippage and taxes are not modelled, and a basket of 3–5 stocks concentrates risk far more than the index it is measured against. With 44.4 lakh combinations tested, the very top rows are partly luck — that is exactly why the ⭐ "best in ALL phases / ALL years" cards exist. This is an unlisted research page, not advice.
