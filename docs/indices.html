How these numbers are made

Level and Today are live from NSE's index feed in market hours; indices the live feed does not carry are marked CLOSE and read from NSE's daily closing file. 1W / 1M / 1Y come from NSE's index publication where it has them; 3M / 6M, every rewound figure and the CLOSE rows are computed from closing values (the close 7 / 30 / 91 / 182 / 365 calendar days earlier, or the last one before that, at most 10 days older) and never stitch across an index rename. PE, PB and dividend yield are NSE's own; the small number beside PE / PB is the valuation percentile — where today's figure sits in that index's own observed range (100 = dearest seen). 52W range is the lowest and highest level of the last year; RS·1M is the index's 1-month return minus Nifty 50's.

Members are NSE's list for the index — today's list, or with Rewind the list in force on that date. Member prices are split / bonus adjusted closes, plus the live price in market hours; a member whose ticker has no price on file shows a dash. The chart draws daily bars from NSE's closing file; dates with no open / high / low on file are drawn as a flat tick at the close. Grouping is a display convenience.
