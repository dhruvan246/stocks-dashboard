Level and 1D % are live from NSE's index feed; 1M / 1Y come from NSE's own index-level publication (which also supplies PE / PB / dividend yield) and fall back to our stored history. 1W and the 30-day sparkline are built from a daily-close window that grows one trading evening at a time — a column reads em-dash until it has enough history, and never stitches across an NSE index rename. Valuation percentile is where today's PE (or PB) sits within that index's own observed range — high (red) = more expensive than usual, low (green) = cheaper; it sharpens as history accrues. 52W shows where the level sits between its 52-week low and high. RS is the index's 1-month return minus Nifty 50's (positive = leading the market). PE/PB/dividend yield aren't published for every index (bond, G-Sec and some factor indices omit them). Grouping is a display convenience, not a data value. Educational only — not investment advice.
