What this page shows

A backtest looks backwards; this looks forwards. Every day a robot writes down each saved strategy's picks at that day's real closing prices and holds them like a real portfolio (rotating at the strategy's own rebalance rhythm). No hindsight, no curve-fitting — this is the honest out-of-sample number.
