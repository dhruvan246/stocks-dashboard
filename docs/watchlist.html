How this page works

Star a stock anywhere on the site and it collects here with your note. Notes save by themselves a moment after you stop typing. Price and Chg in the list are live quotes (Yahoo NSE, about 15 minutes delayed, refreshed every minute while the market is open); a symbol Yahoo does not carry stays blank. The chart and the figures under it come from our own daily price files (split and bonus adjusted, dividends not adjusted), so they show the last close on file, not the live price.
