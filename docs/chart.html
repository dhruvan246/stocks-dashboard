One symbol, a ratio (A/B) or a basket (A+B+C). Draw on it; drawings are saved in this browser, per chart, with no limit.

How to use
Symbols: any NSE/BSE symbol on this site (RELIANCE, M&M, BAJAJ-AUTO), or an index: NIFTY, CNX500, BANKNIFTY, CNXPSUBANK, CNXIT, CNXPHARMA, CNXAUTO, CNXMETAL, CNXREALTY, CNXPSE… TradingView-style NSE: / BSE: prefixes are accepted and ignored.
Maths: + - * /, brackets and plain numbers. Put spaces around a minus sign (A - B), because some symbols contain a hyphen.
Drawing: pick a tool, then click two points (or press and drag). Horizontal line and Text need one click. With the pointer tool, click a drawing to select it, drag it or its end points to move it, and press Delete to remove it.
Data: split/bonus-adjusted daily closes. Not available here: currencies, gold/silver, global indices, futures.
