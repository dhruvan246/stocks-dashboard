How to use
Symbols: any NSE/BSE symbol on this site (RELIANCE, M&M, BAJAJ-AUTO), or an index: NIFTY, CNX500, BANKNIFTY, CNXPSUBANK, CNXIT, CNXPHARMA, CNXAUTO, CNXMETAL, CNXREALTY, CNXPSE… TradingView-style NSE: / BSE: prefixes are accepted and ignored.
Maths: + - * /, brackets and plain numbers. Put spaces around a minus sign (A - B), because some symbols contain a hyphen.
Drawing: pick a tool, then click two points (or press and drag). Horizontal line, Vertical line and Text need one click. Ray is a trend line that carries on past its second point. Measure shows the change, the % change, the bars and the days between two points. With the pointer tool, click a drawing to select it, drag it or its end points to move it, and press Delete to remove it. Undo and Redo step through your changes on the chart that is open.
Chart types: Line, Candles, Bars (open-high-low-close) and Heikin Ashi (smoothed candles: the bars drawn are averages, the numbers at the top left stay the real prices). Indicators: up to three moving averages (simple or exponential, any length), Bollinger Bands, RSI and MACD, all worked out on the closes of the bars on screen (daily, weekly or monthly). Volume is offered for a single stock on daily bars and covers the last 400 sessions only.
Compare: open a chart, type another symbol or ratio in the box and press Compare. Both are then drawn as % change from the first bar on screen (up to four at once); remove one under the chart. The % button puts a single chart on the same percent scale.
Keys: + / - zoom, ← / → move, / jump to the symbol box, F full screen, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo a drawing, Esc drop the tool, Delete remove the selected drawing.
Global: USDINR, DXY, US10Y · USOIL (US crude), BRENT, NATGAS, GOLD and SILVER ($/oz), GOLDINR (Rs/10g), SILVERINR (Rs/kg), XAUINRG (Rs/gram), COPPER, ALUMINIUM, BTCUSD · SENSEX — daily closes (most from 2012), so NIFTY/USDINR or NIFTY/USOIL work. TradingView prefixes (TVC:, SP:, FX_IDC:…) are accepted.
World indices (daily open, high, low and close, whole history, refreshed every night from Yahoo): Americas SPX, NASDAQ, NDX, DJI, RUT, NYA, VIX (US), TSX, IBOV, MEXBOL · Europe FTSE, FTSE250, DAX, MDAX, CAC40, STOXX50, STOXX600, AEX, SMI, IBEX, FTSEMIB, BEL20, ATX, PSI, ISEQ, OMXS30, OMXC25, OMXH25, OSEBX, ATHEX, BIST100 · Asia-Pacific NIKKEI, HSI, HSCEI, SHCOMP, SZCOMP, KOSPI, KOSDAQ, TAIEX, STI, KLCI, JCI, ASX200, ALLORDS, NZX50 · Middle East and Africa TA125, TA35, JSE, JSE40. Typing a country (japan, korea, germany) finds its index, and NIFTY/SPX or CNX500/NIKKEI work. They are in their own currency and points. On a ratio with an Indian index each day uses the world index bar of the same calendar date. An Asian index can be a day behind until its close is published. Not available (no daily history at the source): Argentina, Chile, Poland, Hungary, Russia, China CSI 300, Thailand, Philippines, Vietnam, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Dubai, Qatar.
BSE indices and MCX: BSE:METAL, BSE:MIDCAP, BSE:SMLCAP, BSE:POWER, BSE:PSU · MCX futures in rupees, front month 1! and second month 2!: MCX:COPPER1!, MCX:ALUMINIUM1!, MCX:CRUDEOIL1!, MCX:NATURALGAS2!, and the MCX copper index MCX:MCXCOPRDEX · NSE index futures, front month: NSE:NIFTY1!, NSE:BANKNIFTY1! (from NSE's daily F&O file). These come from the BSE and MCX websites, which only answer a browser, so they are refreshed by hand and not every night — the line under the chart shows the last date. A futures series is the contracts joined end to end without adjustment, so the price steps on each roll day; a day the contract did not trade has no bar.
Data: split/bonus-adjusted daily closes. Not available here: other currencies, GIFT Nifty, India money supply, intraday prices.
