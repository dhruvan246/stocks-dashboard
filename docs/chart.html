How to use
Symbols: any NSE/BSE symbol on this site (RELIANCE, M&M, BAJAJ-AUTO), or an index: NIFTY, CNX500, BANKNIFTY, CNXPSUBANK, CNXIT, CNXPHARMA, CNXAUTO, CNXMETAL, CNXREALTY, CNXPSE… TradingView-style NSE: / BSE: prefixes are accepted and ignored.
Maths: + - * /, brackets and plain numbers. Put spaces around a minus sign (A - B), because some symbols contain a hyphen.
Drawing: pick a tool, then click two points (or press and drag). Horizontal line and Text need one click. With the pointer tool, click a drawing to select it, drag it or its end points to move it, and press Delete to remove it.
Global: USDINR, DXY, US10Y · USOIL (US crude), BRENT, NATGAS, GOLD and SILVER ($/oz), GOLDINR (Rs/10g), SILVERINR (Rs/kg), XAUINRG (Rs/gram), COPPER, ALUMINIUM, BTCUSD · SENSEX, SPX, NASDAQ, NDX, DJI, DAX, CAC40, FTSE, AEX, SMI, NIKKEI, HSI, KOSPI — daily closes (most from 2012), so NIFTY/USDINR or CNX500/SPX work. TradingView prefixes (TVC:, SP:, FX_IDC:…) are accepted.
BSE indices and MCX: BSE:METAL, BSE:MIDCAP, BSE:SMLCAP, BSE:POWER, BSE:PSU · MCX futures in rupees, front month 1! and second month 2!: MCX:COPPER1!, MCX:ALUMINIUM1!, MCX:CRUDEOIL1!, MCX:NATURALGAS2!, and the MCX copper index MCX:MCXCOPRDEX. These come from the BSE and MCX websites, which only answer a browser, so they are refreshed by hand and not every night — the line under the chart shows the last date. A futures series is the contracts joined end to end without adjustment, so the price steps on each roll day; a day the contract did not trade has no bar.
Data: split/bonus-adjusted daily closes. Not available here: other currencies, GIFT Nifty, India money supply, intraday prices.
