Rule: start in cash; BUY (lumpsum) when the fund's trailing 5Y CAGR falls below the buy threshold; SELL to cash when it rises above the sell threshold. Compared against buy-&-hold and monthly SIP over the same period.

Backtests use daily NAVs (post data-floor: Direct from 2013, Regular from 2006). All returns are point-to-point on actual NAVs. Caveats: only funds that exist today are here (survivorship bias — results flatter than reality); figures are before exit load & capital-gains tax; past performance isn't predictive. For research, not advice.
