What these backtests do, and their limits

Test any Indian mutual fund on real daily NAVs — lumpsum, SIP, rolling returns, timing, rotation & best-of-breed. Backtests use daily NAVs (post data-floor: Direct from 2013, Regular from 2006). All returns are point-to-point on actual NAVs. Caveats: only funds that exist today are here (survivorship bias — results flatter than reality); figures are before exit load & capital-gains tax; past performance isn't predictive. For research, not advice.
