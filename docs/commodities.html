Commodity Watch

Product prices for every 8-digit HS code India imports or exports (monthly unit price and volume), 697 wholesale-price-index items (monthly) and global spot prices (daily), each mapped to the listed companies that benefit or suffer when the price moves.

Unit prices are value divided by quantity for the month, so a single unusual shipment can move a small code a lot. Rows flagged thin shipped under 30% of their usual quantity. Signals are hypotheses for research, not calls.

India spot (daily, in rupees)

The domestic prints the newspapers quote: steel and metals by city from MetalBook (its data comes from SteelMint), IBJA's bullion fix, the Rubber Board, sugar spot by city, PPAC's metro fuel prices and NMDC's iron ore. Each price is dated the day its source set it: MetalBook reprices items on their own schedule, often days apart, so read the Priced column. Click any row for its history.

Global proxies (daily, Trading Economics)

Futures and contract-for-difference series, so they give direction rather than the Indian price: their "steel" is Shanghai rebar in yuan and HRC is a US benchmark. Sorted by one-month change.

Sources and method

HS trade panel. Ministry of Commerce MEIDB (tradestat.commerce.gov.in), commodity-wise monthly imports and exports at 8-digit HS, value in US$ million and quantity in the code's unit, from January 2018; a month is published about 45 days after it ends. Unit price = value ÷ quantity. The movers table ranks unit-price changes; the "3-mo vs year-ago" window compares the last three months' aggregate price with the same three months a year earlier and is the least noisy.

WPI items. Office of the Economic Adviser (eaindustry.nic.in) monthly index, base 2011-12 = 100, from April 2012, item level (697 items) plus group rows.

Spot. LME cash settlements and London gold/silver via Westmetall (with history), and a daily snapshot of about 30 global futures from Markets Insider that accumulates its own history from 22 Sep 2026.

Signals. For each commodity group in the beneficiary map, every wired series is scored against a threshold (spot +5% in a week, +10% in a month, +20% in a quarter; WPI +3% / +8% / +15% over 1 / 3 / 12 months; trade unit price +10% / +15% / +30% over 1 month / 3 months / smoothed year-on-year, trade sides under US$3 mn a month ignored, one-month jumps only when the quarter agrees). Strength 1.0 = exactly at threshold. The daily research run reads this file first.
