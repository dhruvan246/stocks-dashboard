How these numbers are made

Cash, daily. Cash-segment provisional figures (₹ crore). Net = buy − sell; positive = net buying. The buy / sell split is shown when NSE has published it. Rows marked NSDL are FII figures from NSDL's daily FPI report (equity, stock exchange), which custodians file the next day; they are not the exchange's same-day provisional numbers, and NSDL has no DII figure. The four figures under the chart and the daily charts use the exchange feed only. Source: NSE + NiftyTrader.

Cash, monthly and yearly. Monthly net = sum of daily provisional cash flows in the calendar month (₹ crore). Combined = FII + DII. Nifty close is the month-end close; Chg% is month-on-month. History from Jul 2014. Yearly net = sum of monthly flows in the calendar year; 2014 is partial (Jul–Dec); Nifty close is the year-end close and Chg% is year-on-year. Source: NSE via NiftyTrader.

F&O. Participant positions = open interest (no. of contracts). Lot-adjusted (index & stock futures): NSE has changed lot sizes many times (Nifty 50 → 75 → 25 → 75 …; stock lots are revised too), so raw contract counts from different years aren't comparable. Each day is restated in today's lot sizes: that day's futures open interest per index / per stock (NSE F&O bhavcopy) ÷ today's lot for it, as a share of the day's contracts — one index factor and one stock factor per day, the same for every participant, since NSE doesn't publish which index or stock a participant holds. Stock splits/bonuses are recognised (open contracts re-sized overnight with a matching price move) and never count as a lot change; stocks no longer in F&O keep their own count. The latest day is unchanged. Totals (which include options) are always NSE's raw counts. Idx-Fut net = index-futures long − short; a large negative FII number = net short / bearish positioning. The buy / sell table is FII-only derivative turnover value. Monthly = the last trading day's participant open interest in each month (a snapshot, not a monthly sum); L/S = long ÷ short (<1 = net short). Since 2012. Source: NSE participant-wise OI + FII derivative-stats reports.
