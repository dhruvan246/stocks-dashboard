Source: NSE public-issues data, refreshed twice daily (mornings + weekday evenings, when subscription numbers update). "×" on an open issue = total bids ÷ shares offered so far. Mainboard includes stocks that start trading in the trade-for-trade (BE) series. Current prices & market caps update live from the NSE feed while markets are open, and are baked into the daily snapshot each evening. A row shows "—" only when the feed has no quote for it yet (common for the newest SME listings). SME names show a live price where available but don't get a dedicated stock page. Pre-IPO financials are annual figures from the company's prospectus era — the years before it began filing quarterly results with the exchange; context only, not used in the backtest. The price chart is daily prices since listing with the issue price as a dashed line; a name listed in the last few days may have no daily price file yet. Educational only — not investment advice.
