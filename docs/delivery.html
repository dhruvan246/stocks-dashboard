How these numbers are made

A delivery spike = a stock where the delivered quantity (shares actually taken home, not day-traded) jumped to a multiple of the stock's own 20-session baseline — with delivery ≥30% of volume, ≥₹1 cr delivered and the price up on the day. That combination usually means somebody is accumulating. Repeat stocks adds up each stock's spikes that match your filters; Biggest today is the latest session's largest delivered values, spike or not (only the search box and the minimum value apply to it).

Source: NSE daily full bhavcopy (delivery columns), one file per trading day, refreshed every trading evening (~21:15 IST) plus a next-morning catch-up — missed days self-heal because each date has its own file. Universe: NSE mainboard, market cap ≥ ₹100 cr. Baseline = the stock's own median delivered quantity over the prior 20 sessions (needs ≥10 sessions of history, so recent listings appear after ~2 weeks). Delivered value = close × delivered quantity. Prices in the list are unadjusted — a corporate-action day disqualifies itself via the price filter. The chart is the stock's daily price history from this site's own store. A spike is interest, not proof: check who's buying on the Bulk/Block Deals and Insider Trades pages.
