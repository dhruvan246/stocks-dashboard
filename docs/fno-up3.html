A stock counts when NSE's official close is more than 3% above its previous close (NSE's previous close is already adjusted on split and bonus days). The F&O list for each day is every stock with stock futures trading on NSE that day, read from NSE's own F&O file for that day, using the ticker that traded then. A stock whose ticker changed is matched by its ISIN. A stock with no closing price that day (for example after a merger) is listed under the day and not counted. Source: NSE daily bhavcopies.
