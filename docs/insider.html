How these numbers are made

Every SEBI PIT Reg 7(2) disclosure on NSE — promoters, directors and key employees buying or selling shares of their own company. Source: NSE insider-trading disclosures, refreshed twice daily by sweeping companies in rotation (full market every ~4 days, recently-active names every run — so a disclosure can appear with a few days' lag). Equity-share transactions only. Date = when NSE broadcast the disclosure; insiders must intimate within 2 trading days of the trade. Value is as disclosed; Post % = the person's shareholding after the transaction.

Click a person's name in the middle pane to see all their disclosures. For a stock, net bought = buys − sells over the disclosures matching your filters (pledges, revocations and invocations are counted as filings but not as buys or sells); people counts the different persons who bought or sold. The page opens on Promoters · Market deals only · Last 30 days. The chart is the stock's daily price history from this site's own store. Educational only — not investment advice.
