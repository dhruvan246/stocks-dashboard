Change % is measured on split/bonus/dividend-adjusted closes: 1D = latest close vs the previous trading close, 1W/1M/1Y = latest close vs the adjusted close roughly 7 / 30 / 365 calendar days earlier. 1D/1W/1M paint instantly from the slim recent feed (~8 months); picking 1Y loads the full price history once. From 52W high / low compares the latest close to the stock's own highest / lowest close in the trailing year (blank until a stock has a full year of history). Market-cap bands: Large ≥ ₹20,000 Cr, Mid ₹5,000–20,000 Cr, Small < ₹5,000 Cr. The liquidity floor hides tiny illiquid tickers by minimum market cap (this feed doesn't carry intraday turnover, so we gate on size rather than a rupee-turnover floor).
