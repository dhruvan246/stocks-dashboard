How these numbers are made

Change is measured on split/bonus-adjusted closes (dividends are not adjusted — the ex-dividend drop counts as a move, as on NSE, Moneycontrol and Screener): 1D = latest close vs the previous trading close, or in market hours NSE's last price vs its previous close; 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y = latest close vs the adjusted close roughly 7 / 30 / 91 / 182 / 365 calendar days earlier (a stock listed more recently than that is left out of the window). 1D–6M use the recent price file (~8 months); 1Y and Rewind load the full price history once. From 52W high / low compares the latest close with the stock's own highest / lowest close of the trailing year. The sector figure is the median move of that sector's stocks under the current window, universe, cap band and floor; sectors with fewer than 3 stocks are left out of the list.

SME = SME-platform listings on both exchanges — NSE Emerge and BSE SME (BSE groups M / MT); BSE SME IPO and Nifty SME Emerge are those indices' current constituents from the exchanges' own lists; picking any of them sets the minimum market cap to None, since most SME names are below ₹100 Cr. Market-cap bands: Large ≥ ₹20,000 Cr, Mid ₹5,000–20,000 Cr, Small below ₹5,000 Cr. The list shows the top 300 rows (every row for the SME views); the CSV has all of them. The chart draws daily bars; a stock with no bar file is drawn from its closing prices only. Prices are end-of-day from official NSE bhavcopies; BSE-only names are priced from their BSE listing.
