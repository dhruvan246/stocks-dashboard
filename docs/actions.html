Every announced corporate action on NSE, ordered by ex-date — own the stock before that day to receive the dividend/bonus/split. Source: NSE corporate-actions filings, refreshed every morning and weekday evening (window: last 30 days + next ~75 days — actions are announced a few weeks ahead, so the far end fills in as companies file). To be eligible you must BUY at least one trading day before the ex-date. Yield = announced dividend ÷ latest close (per-share; interim + final may appear as separate rows). The chart is the stock's own daily prices; the arrow marks the first bar on or after an ex-date that has already passed. Educational only — not investment advice.
