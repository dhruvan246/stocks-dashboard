Official private-capex gauges
MoSPI capex-intentions survey
The NSO's forward-looking survey of the private corporate sector (listed and unlisted): weighted aggregate capex by year, per survey round. Rounds are not comparable with each other — different frames and weights.
RBI — capex envisaged from the project pipeline
RBI Bulletin's annual article on private corporate investment: cost of projects sanctioned by banks/FIs each year, and the capex those pipelines (all financing channels) put into each year. Later articles revise earlier years — the latest vintage is shown, earlier ones on hover.

Reality check — published figures vs this page
Every private-capex number we found in the press or official releases, what it actually measures, and the closest equivalent computed from our filings data. Match = same concept and within ~15%. Definition differs = a different measure (gross-block additions, group totals incl. unlisted arms, spectrum/intangibles) — the gap is explained, not hidden. Not comparable = a different concept altogether (project announcements, survey intentions, GDP accounts).
