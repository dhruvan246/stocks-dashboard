How these numbers are made

India's market, valuation, rates, economy and flows — every indicator with its position in history. The small change beside each reading is coloured green when the move is favourable and red when adverse (for VIX, USD/INR, inflation and crude a fall is the favourable direction). Flow and breadth indicators are shown as monthly bars (current month = month to date). Percentile = share of the indicator's whole history at or below the latest reading.

Twelve quarters: each row shaded against its own 12-quarter range — green favourable, red adverse (CPI inverted: cooling inflation reads green). Blank = no reading that quarter. GDP feed paused Nov-24.

Sector rotation: NSE sector & thematic indices, price returns from monthly closes. Dashed line = Nifty 50 — bars past it are beating the market.

Sources: NSE (Nifty, FII/DII cash & derivatives, breadth) · niftyindices (Nifty PE/PB/dividend yield) · RBI via mql5 economic calendar (repo, deposits, M3, forex, bank credit) · MOSPI via mql5 (CPI, WPI, IIP, GDP, trade) · S&P Global PMIs, US Fed rate & US CPI via mql5 · Yahoo Finance (yields, USD/INR, dollar index, global indices, Brent, gold, silver, copper) · FRED (India 10Y). Breadth, earnings pulse (median YoY from our quarterly-results data), market-cap÷GDP and all ratio/derived panels computed in-house. The regime score is a simple percentile blend — a compass, not investment advice.
