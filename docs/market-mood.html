Each point = sum of the month's traded value (turnover) across that month's Nifty 500 constituents (point-in-time membership), from NSE bhavcopy. Pre-2018 turnover is sampled weekly, so those months are scaled to a full-month estimate (average daily turnover × trading days). The 12-month moving average smooths seasonality to show the underlying trend. Breadth uses the same dataset's corp-action-adjusted daily closes (daily data begins Jan-2002, so the series starts once most members' 52-week window has filled, Jan-2003): % above 200-day average and new 52-week highs/lows count each day's point-in-time members against their own trailing 200 / 252 trading sessions. The Nifty 500 / F&O toggle switches the universe — the F&O list is the stocks with listed futures & options on that day (from ~30 names in 2002); DVR share classes are not counted twice, and the coverage line shows how many members traded and had enough history that day.
