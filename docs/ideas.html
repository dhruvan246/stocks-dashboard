How ideas are produced

Universe. Every active BSE equity with market cap between ₹200 cr and ₹2,000 cr on BSE's scrip master, refreshed daily (about 1,180 names, including BSE SME). Suspended groups are excluded. NSE-only SME names are not yet covered because no public market-cap feed exists for them.

Scan. The last 65 BSE bhavcopies give price, volume and turnover features; the day's BSE announcements are classified into order wins, capex, credit-rating actions, results, fund raises, corporate actions, disclosures and deals. Names below ₹25 lakh median daily turnover are down-weighted.

Research. For each candidate the run builds a dossier from primary sources (BSE filings and attachments, annual reports, investor decks and transcripts, shareholding pattern, corporate actions, adjusted price history, industry peers) and writes the idea in a fixed format: the trigger, what the business physically does, a revenue model from a physical driver (capacity, shipments, head-count, orders), the assumed margin and forward earnings, valuation against peers, red flags, what still needs on-the-ground verification, and the list of unknowns.

Scorecard. Each published idea is re-priced daily from BSE history adjusted for bonus and split: return since the call close and the best close since.
