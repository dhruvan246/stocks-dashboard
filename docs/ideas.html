Research notes, not investment advice. Each number carries a source link; where a datum could not be found the note says "unknown" rather than guessing. Small caps in this band are thinly traded: the scorecard measures closing prices, not what a real order would have achieved.

Universe. Every active BSE equity with market cap between ₹200 cr and ₹2,000 cr on BSE's scrip master, refreshed daily (about 1,180 names, including BSE SME). Suspended groups are excluded. On-request deep-dives (SME and main board) sit outside this band.

Scan. The last 65 BSE bhavcopies give price, volume and turnover features; the day's BSE announcements are classified into order wins, capex, credit-rating actions, results, fund raises, corporate actions, disclosures and deals. Names below ₹25 lakh median daily turnover are down-weighted.

Research. For each candidate the run builds a dossier from primary sources (BSE and NSE filings and attachments, annual reports, investor decks and transcripts, shareholding pattern, corporate actions, adjusted price history, industry peers) and writes the idea in a fixed format: the trigger, what the business physically does, a revenue model from a physical driver, the assumed margin and forward earnings, valuation against peers, red flags, what still needs verification, and the unknowns. X (Twitter) and ValuePickr are swept for every name; only data verified against a filing enters the note.

Kept current. Every evening the run reads each published company's new BSE and NSE filings and appends them to the company's page as dated updates — results with a measured GOOD / AVERAGE / WEAK verdict, orders against a year of sales, fund-raises, management changes, notices. Good news is green, red flags are red. The revenue-guidance line is re-checked against each new call or presentation.

Scorecard. Each published idea is re-priced daily from BSE / NSE history adjusted for bonus and split: return since the call close and the best close since.
