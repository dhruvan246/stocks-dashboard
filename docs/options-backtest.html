How this backtest works, and its limits

End-of-day engine. Runs on real NSE F&O bhavcopy data (exchange closes and settles per strike, 2016 → today) at daily granularity: entries and exits fill at the day close, SL/TP are checked once per day (or against the day's option high/low — see “Lot sizes and SL/TP check” in Set-up). Intraday features (entry time, wait & trade, range breakout) are shown but switched off until minute data lands (phase 2). Strikes that did not trade that day are treated as NA, like StockMock.

Picking a strategy on the left loads it and runs it on the dates shown. Saved strategies and baskets are kept in this browser only.
