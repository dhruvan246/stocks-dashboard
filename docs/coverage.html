Red flags

A date is flagged when its coverage sits below half its own neighbourhood median (±6 month-ends, and only where that median is a real population, >200) — or, for a universe with a real membership roll, when it covers under 90% of that date's members while the neighbours clear it by ≥3 points. Both checks require established coverage before the date, so the first month of a ramp is not a crater. This finds holes; it does not diagnose them.
