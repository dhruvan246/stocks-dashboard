Red flags

A date is flagged when its coverage sits below half its own neighbourhood median (±6 month-ends, and only where that median is a real population, >200) — or, for a universe with a real membership roll, when it covers under 90% of that date's members while the neighbours clear it by ≥3 points. Both checks require established coverage before the date, so the first month of a ramp is not a crater. This finds holes; it does not diagnose them. Click a flag to open it above.

How to read this page
Every screening parameter the site holds, counted at every month-end, for every universe. A value is how many of that date's members carried a usable value — decided by the live backtest engine's own factorsAt() / fieldVal(), so it is literally what a strategy could see there. The left list is the families; a family shows its weakest parameter, and ▸ (or Enter) opens the individual parameters underneath. The middle list is that family or parameter at every month-end; the chart draws the same series, and the strip under the chart shows every family at the selected month. Red rings are the smoke detector, not a verdict.
