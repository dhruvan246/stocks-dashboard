How these numbers are made

* Columns marked with an asterisk are annualized (CAGR). 1D–1Y are absolute returns. Data sources: AMFI NAVAll daily file (scheme master + current NAV) and mfapi.in (full NAV history). Growth plans only (no IDCW/dividend options), tagged DIR / REG. Data floor: Direct-plan NAV history starts Jan 2013 (when direct plans were introduced); Regular-plan history starts Apr 2006 (AMFI's daily-NAV archive floor). So a fund older than 2006 shows max ~20 years even on its Regular plan — its true 1990s/2000s launch isn't available in free data. "Since inception" means since that data floor, not the fund's legal launch. Returns shown as "—" mean the fund hasn't existed for that lookback period (e.g. no 10Y return for a 6-year-old fund). A fund tagged stale has an old latest NAV; its short-period returns are suppressed. Side-pocket / Segregated schemes show -100% returns by design. Categories follow Groww's taxonomy (parent group → subcategory). Closed-ended, fixed-maturity (FMP), infrastructure-debt and other schemes Groww doesn't list are grouped under Uncategorized. The “Avg” beside a category is the plain average of the chosen period's return over its funds that have one, under the current Plan / Min years / search filters. The NAV chart, the custom window and the monthly / trailing / forward calendars read the daily NAV history (about 8 MB), which loads the first time one of them is needed.
