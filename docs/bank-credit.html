Bank credit growth% y/y · fortnightly · RBI

Bank Loan Growth (year-on-year) — the change in total outstanding bank credit of scheduled commercial banks, published fortnightly by the Reserve Bank of India. The same indicator Investing.com tracks: a read on the credit cycle and the broad market regime.

What it measures. The year-on-year change in the total value of outstanding loans issued by banks to households and businesses. The RBI compiles it fortnightly from ~39 commercial banks that cover about 90% of all bank credit.

Why investors watch it. Borrowing and spending move together with confidence in the economy. Faster credit growth means companies and consumers are taking more loans to invest and spend — usually a tailwind for earnings and for banks/NBFCs. A sharp slowdown often flags caution in the real economy. It's a classic gauge of the credit cycle: long expansions (e.g. 2021→2024) tend to coincide with strong equity regimes, while down-shifts (e.g. the 2016→2017 slide to ~4%) warn of a cooling cycle. Turn on the Nifty 50 overlay to eyeball how the credit cycle has lined up with the index.

Source: Reserve Bank of India — Bank Loan Growth (y/y), scheduled commercial banks, fortnightly.
