What is Bank Loan Growth — and why it matters

What it measures. The year-on-year change in the total value of outstanding loans issued by banks to households and businesses. The RBI compiles it fortnightly from ~39 commercial banks that cover about 90% of all bank credit.

Why investors watch it. Borrowing and spending move together with confidence in the economy. Faster credit growth means companies and consumers are taking more loans to invest and spend — usually a tailwind for earnings and for banks/NBFCs. A sharp slowdown often flags caution in the real economy. It's a classic gauge of the credit cycle: long expansions (e.g. 2021→2024) tend to coincide with strong equity regimes, while down-shifts (e.g. the 2016→2017 slide to ~4%) warn of a cooling cycle.
