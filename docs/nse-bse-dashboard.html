How these numbers are made

Change % compares two closing prices: the last close before the From date and the last close on or before the To date. So From = To shows that one day's move against the previous trading day, and "Latest" is the newest trading day in our data. A stock whose only price inside the window is its first trading day shows DAY 1 and no change. STALE means the stock's last trade in the window was more than 14 days before your To date. "since Mar 2024" in amber means the exchange has had no trade since then — the price is the last traded price, and such stocks are left out of gainers, losers and the average.

Index and F&O filters use the list in force on the From date, not today's list. Market cap is today's, in ₹ crore, from the exchanges. From 52W high is how far the latest price sits below the highest price of the last 52 weeks (0% = at the high). The industry average is the plain average of the stocks in it.

Backtest ranks the stocks that pass the filters by their From → To change, buys the top N in equal amounts at the To-date close and sells at the Hold-until close. No costs, taxes or dividends are counted.

Prices are split and bonus adjusted closes from the NSE and BSE daily files (weekly closes 1996–2019, daily 2020 → today). Dividends are not adjusted. Educational only — not investment advice.
