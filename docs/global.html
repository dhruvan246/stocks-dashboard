How these numbers are made

What moved overnight — US, Europe & Asia indices, gold/silver/crude, crypto and the dollar/yields that set the tone for today's Indian open. Every move weighted by how it actually tracks the Nifty.

Data: Yahoo Finance daily closes. Correlations are Pearson of ~1y of daily returns vs the Nifty 50. Overseas markets show their last available close (marked ° when their session is behind ours), which is exactly the cue Indian traders read pre-open. Not investment advice.

Global cue: each overseas market's 1-day move is weighted by its correlation to the Nifty (only positive correlations count; India's own indices are left out). A weighted move above about +0.4% reads risk-on, below about −0.4% risk-off. Chart: “60 sessions” is the trend line carried in this page's own feed (it has no dates). The longer ranges use the daily closes kept for the Macro page, where that instrument has them; the feed's own last close is placed at the end.
