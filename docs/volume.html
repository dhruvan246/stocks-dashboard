How these numbers are made

Stocks trading many times their own 20-session average volume today — often the first sign someone is suddenly interested. Every spike is paired with delivery % (real accumulation vs intraday churn), sector, F&O status and distance from the 52-week high, so you can tell a breakout from a blow-off. Delivery-confirmed = delivery % ≥ 50. Repeat · 5 sessions lists stocks with at least 2 shock sessions in the last 5. The sector line counts today's shockers by sector (green = how many of them were on up days); click a sector to filter the list.

Live now appears only during market hours: it is NSE's own intraday volume-spurt feed, where × = today's volume vs NSE's 1-week average — a different window from the end-of-day list, which uses our 20-session baseline, so the two are never mixed. It refreshes every minute and shows the first 15 names at 2× or more.

Source: NSE daily full bhavcopy (one file per trading day), refreshed every trading evening (~21:15 IST) plus a next-morning catch-up — missed days self-heal because each date has its own file. Ratio = today's traded volume ÷ the mean of the stock's own prior 20 sessions (needs ≥15 sessions of history, so fresh listings appear after ~3 weeks and never inflate their own baseline). Turnover and volume are real NSE columns; delivery % is the share of volume that settled into a demat account rather than squaring off intraday. The chart is the stock's daily price history from this site's own store. A volume shock is interest, not proof — cross-check who's buying on the Bulk/Block Deals and Insider Trades pages.
