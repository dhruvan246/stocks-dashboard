Source: NSE daily full bhavcopy (one file per trading day), refreshed every trading evening (~21:15 IST) plus a next-morning catch-up — missed days self-heal because each date has its own file. Ratio = today's traded volume ÷ the mean of the stock's own prior 20 sessions (needs ≥15 sessions of history, so fresh listings appear after ~3 weeks and never inflate their own baseline). Turnover and volume are real NSE columns; delivery % is the share of volume that settled into a demat account rather than squaring off intraday. A volume shock is interest, not proof — cross-check who's buying on the Bulk/Block Deals and Insider Trades pages. Educational only, not investment advice.
