How these buckets are made

Stocks grouped by what's actually happening to them — order wins, fund raises, great or weak results, breakouts, sector themes. Every bucket is computed automatically from exchange filings and market data (nothing hand-picked) and refreshes through the day.

Buckets are screens computed from public NSE/BSE data — announcements (last ~31 days), quarterly filings and daily prices. For research & education only, not recommendations. Results buckets: PAT is owners-attributable, consolidated preferred.

The chart, Last close and 1 year come from our own daily price files (split and bonus adjusted, dividends not adjusted); the other figures are the bucket's own.
